# duo-storage-steps

What each iPhone Duo storage upgrade costs, in the store's own currency. It's a small personal reference that I keep so I stop redoing the subtraction.

## data

Launch list prices, before trade-in, as recorded for Apple's four stores on 2026-09-13:

tier    US (USD)   UK (GBP)   HK (HKD)   CN (RMB)
256GB   1,999      1,999      17,499     15,999
512GB   2,199      2,199      19,299     17,999
1TB     2,599      2,599      22,799     21,499
2TB     3,199      3,199      27,999     26,499

## steps (same row, same currency)

step            +GB    US/UK   HK       CN
256GB -> 512GB  256    +200    +1,800   +2,000
512GB -> 1TB    512    +400    +3,500   +3,500
1TB   -> 2TB    1024   +600    +5,200   +5,000

## per extra GB (rounded, local currency)

step            US/UK   HK      CN
256GB -> 512GB  ~0.78   ~7.03   ~7.81
512GB -> 1TB    ~0.78   ~6.84   ~6.84
1TB   -> 2TB    ~0.59   ~5.08   ~4.88

In every store, the largest step costs the least per extra gigabyte and the most in absolute terms. That's only a reason to buy 2TB if you would actually fill it.

## caveats

  • Columns are different currencies. Don't compare digits across columns. US and UK share numerals, and that tells you nothing about which costs less.
  • The prices are a dated snapshot. Check the live store before you rely on them.
  • Trade-in, financing, AppleCare+ and accessories are left out on purpose.
  • Per-GB maths treats 1TB as 1,024GB. Using 1,000GB moves the last digits slightly and leaves the order unchanged.
  • A cheaper store only helps if the phone can activate your number there. The mainland China activation list is A3518, A3635, A3715, A3718, A3721.

## source

Prices transcribed from the regional comparison table at iphoneduo.io, which links each column to the Apple store it came from. The step and per-GB tables are my own arithmetic.
